Day 1 · Tuesday, November 24
Matcha, Snoopy, and a warm Sonoma night
8:20–10:00 AM

Drive from Mountain View to Stonemill Matcha

Address: 561 Valencia Street, San Francisco, CA. Plan 8:20 AM departure with a traffic buffer; Stonemill opens 10:00 AM Tuesdays. Eat a Japanese-inspired brunch and enjoy matcha. It's better not to schedule anything before opening time.

Stonemill official website · Directions

10:40 AM–12:50 PM

Drive north; Golden Gate Bridge optional

Take US-101 north. The Golden Gate Bridge itself is on the route, but pulling into north Vista Point adds parking time. Stop for 10–15 minutes only if visibility is good and you're on schedule. If the Tuesday rain forecast holds or Stonemill takes longer, skip the stop so Snoopy's remains relaxed.

North Vista Point directions

12:50–2:30 PM

Snoopy's Home Ice & Warm Puppy Café — MUST DO

Address: 1667 West Steele Lane, Santa Rosa, CA. Public skating is listed 12:30–3:30 PM on Tuesday, November 24. Arrive around 12:45–1:00 PM, skate about 60 minutes, then enjoy hot chocolate, snacks and a browse at the gift shop. Adult holiday admission is listed at $14 plus $4 for skate rental; tickets are bought onsite. The Charles M. Schulz Museum is normally closed Tuesdays, so the café + rink are the best combination.

2:00–2:35 PM

Warm Puppy Café, if you haven't stopped already

Have hot cocoa beside the rink's signature fireplace, which is part of the Snoopy experience—not a separate restaurant detour.

Official ice rink schedule · Warm Puppy Café

2:35–3:30/3:45 PM

Drive to Fairmont Sonoma, check in

Recommended two-night base: Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa, 100 Boyes Boulevard, Sonoma. Plan 45–65 minutes from the rink depending on traffic. Check-in normally begins around 4 PM; early room access is not guaranteed.

3:45–5:00 PM

Optional geothermal bathhouse soak

The indoor Roman-style geothermal bathhouse is real, and the adults-only Fairmont Spa lists Tuesday 10 AM–5 PM hours. This means spa admission only makes sense if you arrive early enough and arrange it beforehand. Ask the hotel to confirm the November 24 schedule, bathhouse-only access, price, and whether your Chase The Edit property credit can apply. The separate main geothermal resort pool is generally included with the stay and may be a better choice if you arrive too late; confirm its evening hours.

Fairmont spa hours · Pool access information

6:30–7:45 PM

Dinner in Sonoma: El Dorado Kitchen

First choice: El Dorado Kitchen, 405 1st Street West, Sonoma. Warm, social, polished but relaxed; right on Sonoma Plaza for the walk afterward. Reservation availability for two was found at 6:30 PM Tuesday, Nov 24 when checked; book to secure it.

Backup choices: LaSalette (Portuguese-inspired, also confirmed dinner availability) and the girl & the fig (French bistro; only an 8 PM opening surfaced in the checked window). Dinner reservations are not yet made.

El Dorado Kitchen reservations · LaSalette restaurant · the girl & the fig

7:45–8:30 PM

Sonoma Plaza walk / Fairmont firepits

Walk the historic square and surrounding illuminated streets after dinner, if it's not pouring. Otherwise, return to the hotel's fireside seating or main pool. This is the warm and lively evening you wanted—not a long isolated night hike.

Sonoma Plaza directions

Day 2 · Wednesday, November 25
Through the vineyards to the holiday lights
7:45–9:50 AM

Hotel breakfast and drive to Calistoga

Enjoy a proper hotel breakfast before leaving around 8:45 AM. Allow roughly 60–75 minutes for the drive to Calistoga, depending on route and weather. No extra stops: this is your day for biking, village walking and the winery.

10:00–11:30 AM

Tandem bicycle ride through Napa vineyards

At Calistoga Bikeshop, 1318 Lincoln Avenue, reserve a two-person tandem bike (listed at $135/day) or choose two ordinary bikes. The paved Napa Valley Vine Trail runs south toward St. Helena through wine-country scenery. For a first tandem ride, practice together, then do roughly 4–6 miles round trip, with photo stops. After Tuesday rain, wet pavement is the main reason to shorten or skip biking.

Calistoga Bikeshop tandem reservation · Vine Trail information

11:30–11:45 AM

Short stroll on Calistoga's Lincoln Avenue

Return the bike and enjoy the little shops, café windows and historic small-town atmosphere. This is your first Napa village stop—short and casual, not a separate drive.

11:45 AM–12:30 PM

Lunch at Sam's Social Club

Sam's Social Club, 1712 Lincoln Avenue, is a lovely, lively sit-down lunch setting with a garden patio. Its weekday lunch service is published 11 AM–2:30 PM. For a simpler schedule, order a sandwich or share something light, and leave by 12:30 PM.

Menu and hours · Directions

12:45–2:30 PM

Guided castle/winery tour — MUST RESERVE

Castello di Amorosa, 4045 St. Helena Highway, Calistoga. Target a 1:00 PM guided castle tour or the nearest available reservation. You both don't drink, so the architecture, vineyards, cellars and storytelling matter more than a tasting. The winery explicitly permits guests to decline wine and offers nonalcoholic options; reservations are still required for entry, even just to look around. Arrive around 15 minutes before the tour.

Castle visit & booking · No-wine tasting policy

2:45–3:30 PM

Optional easy redwood hike

Bothe-Napa Valley State Park, 3801 St. Helena Highway. Walk part of the Redwood Trail / Ritchey Canyon creekside route for 30–45 minutes, then turn around. The woods provide a lovely change of scenery from the vineyards. Skip the hike first if your tour runs late, you're tired, or the trails are muddy after Tuesday rain.

Park information

3:45–6:15 PM

St. Helena village and Wine Barrel Tree Lighting

Arrive at historic St. Helena Main Street for shop browsing and coffee, then attend the annual Wine Barrel Tree Lighting on Hunt Avenue, 5:00–7:00 PM. It is a genuine Nov 25, 2026 holiday event, with lights, music, a market and simulated snowfall. This is a much better second village experience than adding a drive down to downtown Napa.

Official 2026 lighting event

6:30–7:45 PM

Dinner at Market, St. Helena

Market, 1347 Main Street, is a lively, warm American/California bistro right in the village. A table for two at 6:30 PM Wednesday, Nov 25 was available when checked. If you'd prefer Italian, Cook St. Helena had later bookings around 7:30 PM. Book soon, since this is Thanksgiving Eve.

Market reservation page · Cook St. Helena

7:45–9:00 PM

Drive back to Sonoma

Plan about 60–75 minutes to return to Fairmont, possibly more in the rain. No further activities tonight: enjoy the room or a brief resort stroll. Pack for an early Point Reyes departure.

Day 3 · Thanksgiving Thursday, November 26
Elk on the wild coast, then dinner at home

Hard deadline: Be at Zazie, 941 Cole Street, San Francisco, no later than 3:30 PM for its 3–4 PM pickup (if your preorder is confirmed). The coastal segment is the flexible part—not the pickup.

6:45–7:30 AM

Hotel breakfast, checkout, depart

Get an early breakfast if available, or have something quick from the hotel. Leave by 7:30 AM. Allow approximately 1h45–2h15 to drive to the Tomales Point Trailhead at Pierce Point Ranch in Point Reyes. Take an extra layer: the coast can be colder and windier than Sonoma.

9:30–11:00 AM

Tomales Point tule elk and ocean-view hike

Tomales Point Trail to Windy Gap, at the northern end of Pierce Point Road. Hike about 2 miles round trip (roughly 60–90 minutes including elk watching). The National Park Service confirms views of the Pacific and Tomales Bay and identifies this as prime tule elk habitat. Wildlife is not guaranteed. Do not approach elk or go near unstable cliff edges.

NPS Windy Gap hike · Elk viewing information

Skip these detours today: Point Reyes Lighthouse, Cypress Tree Tunnel and Sonoma Coast State Park. They are beautiful, but adding them to the same morning makes your pickup less dependable. If it's very windy or rainy, do a shorter Pierce Point Road elk viewpoint stop instead of the full hike.

11:00 AM–12:30 PM

Depart Point Reyes toward Marin

A packed snack, water and a thermos are useful. Head toward Fairfax / San Anselmo and do not plan a second coastal hike. If you're behind schedule, skip the sit-down lunch and go directly to SF.

12:30–1:10 PM

Lunch at Creekside Pizza & Taproom, San Anselmo

Creekside Pizza & Taproom, 638 San Anselmo Avenue. A local, casual place for pizza, sandwiches, salads or a quick to-go meal. Crucially, the restaurant's own 2026 holiday calendar explicitly says it is open Thanksgiving Day noon–5 PM. Keep lunch to around 30–45 minutes.

Zazie Thanksgiving preorder pickup — MUST DO

Reservations, hotel choices & practical notes

Accommodation verdict

Book Fairmont Sonoma Mission Inn & Spa for Nov 24–26 if a good room category and acceptable The Edit rate are available. It is geographically sound for all three days and the strongest rainy-day option: a full-service, active resort, indoor/outdoor geothermal mineral bathing, fireplace lounges and nighttime grounds for a cozy walk. It is about a 10-minute drive to Sonoma Plaza, so use the shuttle when its schedule fits. Room choice matters: consider a Deluxe King with Fireplace rather than the historic entry-level room if freshness is important.

Runner-up: MacArthur Place Hotel & Spa. This has the newer-looking rooms and more walkable garden/village atmosphere; it features a conventional heated pool and whirlpool (normally open 7 AM–10 PM), not natural geothermal springs. Choose it if room design, direct walking access to the Plaza and a night hot-tub soak outweigh the genuine springs.

The Edit note: only book if the Chase Travel checkout explicitly labels the rate The Edit and shows your benefits. Its standard $100 property credit is per stay, not per day; verify which room charges and spa access fees qualify.
